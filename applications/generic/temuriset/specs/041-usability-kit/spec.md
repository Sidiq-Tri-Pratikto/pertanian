## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Learn from 5–8 people without inventing results (Priority: P1)

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the kit, **When** a facilitator runs a session, **Then** neutral task scripts for inventors, company managers and partners, consent text, observation sheets and a results template are ready.
2. **Given** results, **When** recorded, **Then** completion, errors, assistance, time and understanding that approval is not funding are captured, with denominators, and nothing is claimed beyond the sample.
