## Task outline

1. Use the 040 inventory and PRD section 20 to plan 5–8 formative participants across inventors, company managers and partners. Define the synthetic setup, keyboard/mobile/assistive-technology conditions and a route for inaccessible or blocked tasks.
2. Write neutral Indonesian participant prompts and consent text. Keep setup, moderator checkpoints and expected outcomes separate. Require approved storage, access and retention before handling any participant data.
3. Provide blank observation and results templates with timing rules, assistance/error coding and denominators. Include the approval-versus-funding comprehension prompt. Use the product targets only as hypotheses for a later real study.
4. Verify the kit internally with synthetic fixtures. Preserve future J04/J05 tasks in the kit as gated and leave real participant results blank until an approved study runs.
