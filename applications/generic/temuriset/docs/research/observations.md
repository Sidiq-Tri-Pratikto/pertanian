## Coding

- **Unassisted** means the participant independently reaches the observable success state. Reading the standardized prompt or providing a prearranged accommodation counts as setup.
- **Assisted** means the participant reaches the success state after moderator help with how to perform the task. Record the cue and when it was given.
- **Failed** means a runnable task was attempted and the success state was not reached. Record the errors, abandonment or agreed time limit.
- **Skipped** means an otherwise available task was declined, omitted by design or left unattempted. Record the reason.
- **Blocked** means the required build, fixture, permissions, route or accommodation prevented a valid attempt. Record the dependency or access barrier separately, even when the participant made some progress.

Use a monotonic timer. For general tasks start when the participant finishes reading the prompt and can act. Stop on the verified success state, voluntary abandonment or a predeclared time limit. I2 starts with a prepared profile and need at the agreed launch route, and stops at visible confirmation of the addressed pitch. F1 starts with an available scheduling view and the scenario's permitted slots, and stops when the participant selects an available slot and can identify its confirmation. Record any moderator interruption, technical wait or invalid timing separately. Keep raw elapsed times and document any exclusions in the results.
