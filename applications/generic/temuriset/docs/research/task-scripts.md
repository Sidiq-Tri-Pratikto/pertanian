# Task scripts

Read the prompt and provide only the synthetic facts the participant needs. Keep the rubric and assistance cues on the facilitator's copy.

| ID | Prompt to read |
|---|---|
| I1 | “Anda sedang menyiapkan uji lapangan saringan air. Biaya uji enam bulan sekitar USD 20.000 sampai 30.000. Temukan cara agar calon mitra dapat menilai apakah kebutuhan uji ini sesuai dengan mandat mereka.” |
| I2 | “Profil dan kebutuhan uji Anda sudah siap. Anda ingin menyampaikan ajakan membahas uji itu kepada Mitra Validasi. Tunjukkan bagaimana Anda memastikan ajakan terkirim ke pihak yang Anda pilih.” |
| C1 | “Perusahaan Anda mengembangkan LoopMaterial dan ReFormPress dengan satu rencana pendanaan. Cari informasi yang dapat Anda tunjukkan kepada rekan untuk menjelaskan siapa yang mencari pendanaan dan bagaimana kedua inovasi terkait.” |
| C2 | “Anda ingin mengirim ajakan kepada investor yang mandatnya sesuai dengan rencana perusahaan. Setelah itu, dari inovasi kedua, cari kembali ajakan yang sama.” |
| P1 | “Anda menerima ajakan untuk membahas uji lapangan. Rincian biaya untuk dua lokasi belum jelas. Tunjukkan bagaimana Anda meminta penjelasan agar pengirim tahu bagian yang perlu diperbaiki.” |
| P2 | “Anda sudah menerima jawaban revisi dan ingin membicarakan langkah berikutnya dengan pengirim. Lakukan tindakan yang menurut Anda sesuai.” |
| F1 | “Kedua pihak ingin bertemu. Anda tersedia pada Selasa pukul 10.00 WIB dan Rabu pukul 14.00 WIB. Tentukan satu pilihan yang bisa dipakai bersama.” |
| F2 | “Kedua pihak sedang meninjau ringkasan hubungan untuk ditampilkan kepada publik. Anda setuju menampilkan judul dan tujuan, tetapi ingin menjaga rincian biaya tetap privat. Tunjukkan bagaimana Anda akan menanganinya.” |

After I2, C2 or P2, ask once without leading the participant.

> “Menurut Anda, apa yang terjadi setelah ajakan ini disetujui? Apa yang dapat dan belum dapat dilakukan kedua pihak?”

## Facilitator rubric

| ID | Observable success | Optional assistance after an unaided attempt |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | Correct currency, minimum/maximum and six-month period saved on the project need. Returns to the match and can identify the resulting state. | Ask where the project need lives. |
| I2 | Correct source, recipient and brief reviewed. A single submission is confirmed and the participant can identify who has the next turn. | Ask what evidence would tell them the request was sent. |
| C1 | Identifies the company as the funding subject and identifies both linked inventions in the same raise. | Invite the participant to look for information the two inventions share. |
| C2 | Sends one pitch against the company raise and opens the existing pitch from the second linked invention. | Ask what record they expect to find again. |
| P1 | Sends a required, itemized request that identifies the missing budget detail and reason. | Ask what information would help the sender respond. |
| P2 | Chooses the action that opens discussion after review, and identifies the resulting state. | Ask where the result of their decision would appear. |
| F1 | Chooses an available slot in the right time zone and identifies confirmation or conflict from the UI. | Ask which times the scenario permits. |
| F2 | Reviews the correct version and selects only the permitted public fields. Recognizes that both sides must confirm the same version. | Ask which fields they intended to show. |

Score comprehension independently. Code correct when the participant explains in their own words that approval opens discussion and leaves a funding commitment undecided. Code incorrect when they treat approval as a commitment or transfer. Record unclear when the answer is ambiguous and one neutral follow-up still leaves it unresolved. Record skipped or blocked when no answer can be collected. Wait to explain the expected meaning until after recording the answer.
