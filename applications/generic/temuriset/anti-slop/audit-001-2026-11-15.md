# antislop audit 001: TemuRiset web UI

Date: 15 November 2026. Branch `f/047-ui-antislop`, cut from `f/046-investor-digest`. Mode: after (review, then fix).

## Design Read

Reading this as: a signed-in work app for Indonesian inventors and investors, in the calm, plain-language style of the TemuRiset Design System 1.0.0-draft (slate canvas, white surfaces, teal only for the one primary action, system sans, own stroke icons, light theme only), dial **ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1**.

Direction comes from `docs/generic/temuriset/design-system/project/README.md`. It asks for no big hero areas, no decorative dashboards, one primary action per task, and `NextStep` at the top of every workspace. This audit applies the filter on top of that direction; it does not restyle it.

## What judges and users reward

- Hackathon rubrics usually score 4 or 5 criteria: innovation, technical execution, impact, presentation and design or UX. A working, stable demo beats an ambitious unfinished one, and one weak area drags the whole score down. Judges ask whether a first-time user can use it without training, and whether it can continue after the event.
- Indonesia's research downstreaming ("hilirisasi") is a current national priority. BRIN described Rumah Inovasi Indonesia in December 2025 as a hub matching innovators with industry and investors, and names the "valley of death" between publication and commercialization. TemuRiset answers the next question: once matched, how does a pitch get reviewed, revised, scheduled and confirmed honestly?
- Comparable platforms (Flintbox, IN-PART, UArizona StartupHub, OSU RocketTechs) are catalogues. StartupHub's "meet the inventor" videos stand out because they show motivation, not only specs.
- Onboarding research agrees on three things this app needed: the empty state is the first onboarding moment and must offer the action that fills it; reveal settings only when relevant; segment people early and tailor what they see.

## Findings

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate is HIGH, Purpose-Gate is MEDIUM, Quality Locks are LOW.

Checked and kept, with the reason:

- Arrow icon on eight primary buttons (R-08): it appears only on actions that move to the next step (send code, continue, send pitch), never on every button.
- "Versi awal" tag beside the wordmark (R-09): honest provenance that the release is early, which the design system asks for.
- Light theme only (R-21): an owner decision in the design system README, not a default.
- Shadows (R-12): only on `Dialog`, as the design system specifies. No glow, glass, gradients or background patterns anywhere.
- Fixture names such as "Investor B" and "Mitra A" (R-18, R-38): every demo screen carries "Demo · Data fiktif", and real deployments never show them.

## Delivery Gate

Block 1, Hard Gate:

- R-02 PASS: no em dash in UI text after #9 (`grep "—"` over `src` finds none).
- R-03 PASS: Beranda at 375 px has `scrollWidth` 375; `make e2e` checks overflow, 200% text and 320 px reflow on every route.
- R-17, R-18, R-36, R-38 PASS: no invented statistics or testimonials; demo data is labelled on every screen; match ranges come from the scoring service.
- R-23 PASS: no logo or photo was created; the wordmark stays the word "TemuRiset".
- R-24, R-26 PASS: the empty "Segera hadir" heading is gone; every new action links to an existing route (`/inventions`, `/discover`, `/matches?source=…`).
- R-25 PASS: only design-system tokens are used; their contrast is validated in the design system, and `make e2e` runs axe in Chromium.
- R-27 PASS: Beranda now has empty-with-action, next-step and failure states.
- R-32, R-39 PASS: the disclosure is native `details`; new controls are native links and buttons.
- R-33 PASS: every change is in source.
- R-34 PASS: light theme only, by direction.
- R-35 PASS: clicked through in the browser: demo picker shows grouped accounts; "Masuk sebagai Rani" opens Beranda; "Lihat kecocokan" opens Kecocokan with "Siapkan pitching"; phone width shows no overflow. Second pass: Discover at 1280 px shows filters left and the first results above the fold; Kecocokan reads "88–95 dari 100 · informasi 95%"; Beranda header is 147 px at 375 px and 177 px at 320 px with no horizontal scroll; no console errors on these pages. Console errors seen were from manual probes (sign-out while signed out, sign-in before seeding), not from the pages.
- R-37 PASS: direction is the TemuRiset Design System.
- R-40 PASS: no new media; no performance number is claimed (not measured).

Block 2, Purpose-Gate: PASS on every item; see "Checked and kept" for the arrow icon and badge reasons.

Block 3, Liveliness: dials declared above. The focal point on Beranda is now `NextStep`. The one accent is teal on the primary action and the your-turn band. The identity motif is the "Menunggu Anda / Menunggu pihak lain" turn language with its band.

Block 4, Craftsmanship: PASS. The layout is the product's own (NextStep, task, aside), not a landing or dashboard template.

## Hackathon demo path

1. Open `/start`. The demo picker is first. Say: "Two personas, no company or patent required."
2. "Masuk sebagai Rani". Beranda says what to do next: "Temukan investor atau mitra untuk kebutuhan Anda". Open "Lihat kecocokan": three plain reasons and the match range.
3. "Siapkan pitching", review what is shared, send.
4. Sign out, "Masuk sebagai Peserta P": the pitch waits in Pitching Masuk. Ask for one revision or approve for discussion, propose times.
5. Show the honesty rules on screen: UNKNOWN is not FAIL, approval is not funding, documents name one recipient and version.
6. Close with the investor quick subscription at `/langganan`: an investor gives an email and topics and gets a reasoned alert.
