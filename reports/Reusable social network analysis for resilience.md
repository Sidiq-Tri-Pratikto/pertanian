# Reusable social network analysis for resilience

**Recommend CascadeLens: a reusable workbench for replaying observed information-sharing relationships, with evidence for every edge and explicit limits on what the network reveals.** Choose it only after obtaining permitted, timestamped interaction records; otherwise, select **RelayResilience**, using an opt-in organisational collaboration network. This follows your preference for a cross-sector product with national defence as one application. Indonesia already has commercial SNA tools and publicly documented TNI use, so neither a network map nor a defence label establishes novelty. Foreign defence research supports information-flow analysis, organisational coordination and missing-data robustness, but the evidence mixes trials, studies and research programmes. Five product alternatives follow, each with a defined decision, graph, data path and evaluation. Findings reflect public sources reviewed through **26 October 2026, WIB**; proposed benefits and technical designs have not been tested.

## Indonesia already uses SNA, so the product needs a narrower job

Social Network Analysis studies relationships among actors and the structure those relationships produce. For a reusable product, the critical question is what an edge records: a reply, repost, declared affiliation, validated collaboration or acknowledged handoff. A shared keyword or document co-mention creates a different kind of association. An account graph, organisation graph and semantic knowledge graph can all be useful, but their conclusions are not interchangeable. The change from the earlier sentiment topic is therefore substantive: text classification becomes optional, while relationship fidelity and observation boundaries become central.

Kartika's official website explicitly lists **Social Network Analysis as an example under Siber Sosial**, alongside sentiment dashboards and other social-cyber solutions. Its rubric allocates **25% relevance/benefit, 25% technical innovation, 25% prototype functionality, 15% implementation feasibility and 10% documentation**; relevance refers to TNI AD IT/OT security needs. A generic architecture with a credible public-resilience demonstration is a product recommendation, not an organiser exemption from that criterion. Submission is **15 November 2026**. The site still conflicts on final dates, **20 versus 24 November**. Public material did not establish the complete dataset, originality or IP/reuse rules. ([Official competition website](https://kartikacyberhackathon.id/))

**TNI already publicly described SNA activity in October 2023.** Its AIS Forum taskforce report explicitly mentions SNA monitoring; the official indexed article was accessible, although direct retrieval failed. A July 2025 TNI AD statement also calls for personnel to master SNA for publication evaluation. These are institutional evidence of activity and recognised capability, rather than verification of particular software, algorithms, current contracts or accuracy. Consequently, a proposal claiming that defence SNA has not been implemented in Indonesia would contradict available evidence. ([TNI activity report](https://ppid.tni.mil.id/view/32436996/ktt-ais-forum-telah-berakhir-namun-tidak-untuk-pengamanannya.html), [TNI AD statement](https://ppid.tniad.mil.id/index.php/i/26917/kadispenad-insan-penerangan-tni-ad-harus-selalu-update-dan-komunikatif))

Commercial overlap is substantial. NoLimit describes accounts, interactions, conversation communities and propagation in its SNA dashboard. Kazee markets SNA and describes degree, closeness and betweenness. Drone Emprit's COVID19 and New Normal analyses publish concrete network maps. These establish offerings and first-party applications, not independent accuracy or complete platform coverage. Public documentation reviewed did not establish the narrower combination of edge-level evidence, observed/inferred distinctions, correction histories and sensitivity to missing observations. That is a **documentation gap with low confidence as a market gap** until product demonstrations are obtained. ([NoLimit](https://nolimit.id/blog/social-network-analysis-untuk-melihat-hubungan-interaksi-di-sosial-media/), [Kazee product](https://kazee.id/platform/media-intelligence/id), [Kazee methods](https://blog.kazee.id/melacak-pengaruh-pengguna-di-sosial-medi-dengan-social-network-analysis), [Drone Emprit example](https://pers.droneemprit.id/covid19/))

Indonesian research supplies relevant problems without proving vacant markets. A 2026 author study examines KP2C flood-information interactions and cross-community connections; a UGM thesis examines temporal reactions to BMKG earthquake communication. ITB research maps inter-organisational disaster-management collaboration in Cilegon. Together they support questions about dissemination, handoffs and collaboration dependence. They do not establish national rates of coordination failure, supply redistribution-cleared datasets, or show that a proposed dashboard improves outcomes. ([KP2C study](https://ejurnal.undana.ac.id/index/index.php/JIKOM/article/view/9905), [UGM thesis](https://etd.repository.ugm.ac.id/penelitian/detail/181285), [ITB thesis](https://digilib.itb.ac.id/gdl/view/80997/25420011))

## Foreign defence precedents favour evidence and robustness

**Australia's RAPID provides a developed-platform and trial precedent.** A University of Melbourne case study describes work with DSTG modelling interactions among users, posts and topics, with trials involving DSTG and US Army Research Laboratory researchers. Its event comparisons and time sequencing are useful design inspiration. This is a research partner's account of development and trials, not independent evidence of permanent operational adoption or reliable judgments about account authenticity. Borrow inspectable interaction relationships and event comparison, while evaluating our own edge correctness. ([University of Melbourne, January 2023](https://research.unimelb.edu.au/partnerships/case-studies/ai-platform-targets-crowd-sourced-data-for-military-intelligence))

**US DARPA SocialSim is a completed research programme.** It studied the spread and evolution of online information and explicitly identified disaster-relief information delivery as an application. The official page is marked complete and unmaintained. This establishes a dual civilian/defence research rationale; it does not establish accurate behavioural forecasts. An observed-network replay is a feasible starting point without adding speculative diffusion prediction. ([DARPA SocialSim](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/computational-simulation-of-online-social-behavior))

**NATO StratCom COE's January 2024 report documents analytical methods.** Its network section discusses accounts, hashtags and domains, retweet/co-hashtag networks, centrality, Louvain communities and k-core analysis. It is a published study whose views are not NATO policy, rather than a NATO-wide deployed platform. Its transferable lesson is to specify the relationship being analysed and expose the analytical parameters. The broader ambitions of the report are not requirements for a generic SNA product. ([Primary report, network section](https://stratcomcoe.org/publications/download/Revised-AI-in-Support-of-StratCom-Capabilities-DIGITAL---Copy.pdf))

**Canada's Defence R&D applicability study supplies an older methodological precedent.** The 2012 government catalogue abstract describes evaluating SNA against computational, flow/topology and missing-data requirements. This supports reliability testing, not a claim about a current Canadian production system. Supplementary US Army OEVis capability text and a UK multinational organisational experiment were found, but direct/full-text access was incomplete; neither is needed to establish the recommendation. Across the stronger cases, foreign evidence validates relevant analytical questions, not Indonesian absence or commercial demand. ([Canadian government catalogue](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.876621/publication.html))

The common pattern is **a user decision supported by an explainable path**. A relationship database is one implementation choice; the award evidence does not show that the same decision requires Neo4j, an LLM or a graph neural network. Nor does it establish that these products are new to Indonesia. The five alternatives below translate that pattern into distinct reusable jobs.

## Five alternatives make different network decisions

### 1. CascadeLens audits observed information flow

**Decision and Indonesian problem.** A researcher or communication team asks where a particular message travelled through the interactions available to them, and which path claims have supporting records. The Indonesian flood/BMKG studies establish local relevance for temporal dissemination analysis. The narrower hypothesis is that evidence-linked replay reduces errors and review time compared with reading a chronological list. Search finds individual posts; a temporal graph connects documented actions across actors and exposes branching or cross-community links.

**Graph and minimal method.** Nodes are accounts or participating organisations, with content records retained as evidence. Edges are typed, directed and timestamped replies, reposts or explicitly recorded forwards. Declare direction separately by relation: a source-author-to-resharer edge differs from sender-to-reply-recipient. Aggregate repeated events only within a disclosed window and retain originals. Begin with components, degree, time filtering and time-respecting path queries; community detection is optional on a clearly labelled projection. A reply is not endorsement. Root-referencing repost records create an observed star, not evidence of every intermediate exposure step. The earliest item is labelled **first observed in this corpus**, never the true origin.

**MVP and reuse.** Import one permitted event-level CSV; validate identifiers and timestamps; replay one event with an evidence drawer and coverage/exclusion panel. Every displayed path must resolve to its underlying records. A second authorised sharing dataset uses the same schema and interface, proving reuse across owned customer communities, incident announcements, campus information and event coordination. Optional defence use is a retrospective public emergency-information or exercise-announcement replay, inspired by RAPID and SocialSim; no exact military adoption of this proposal is established.

**Competition, evaluation and assumption.** NoLimit already describes propagation/community maps, so the proposed differentiation is traceable temporal edges, explicit unknown links and analyst corrections. Market-gap confidence remains low. Audit source/target, relation, time and direction against sampled records; test impossible temporal paths and compare reconstruction accuracy/time with a table. Show how omitted edges change conclusions. **Choose this only if direct interaction records exist**; similar wording and nearby timestamps cannot substitute for missing transmission evidence. ([NoLimit's existing functionality](https://nolimit.id/blog/social-network-analysis-untuk-melihat-hubungan-interaksi-di-sosial-media/), [temporal-network foundation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1780))

### 2. PatternAudit reviews repeated co-actions

**Decision and Indonesian problem.** Publishers, community operators or organisations auditing their own distribution need to inspect repeated sharing patterns without automatically attributing intent. Kazee already markets SNA and buzzer tracking, so this is not a claim to introduce coordination analytics locally. The proposed improvement is an analyst queue explaining how a repeated pattern differs from ordinary activity and which observations support it. The prevalence of that unmet need in Indonesia remains a validation question.

**Graph and minimal method.** Build a bipartite graph of pseudonymous actors and approved artefacts, such as canonical URLs, with timestamped sharing edges. An actor–actor projection records overlap within a declared window. That is an **inferred similarity edge**, not observed conversation, shared ownership or malicious coordination. Start with one artefact type, conservative URL canonicalisation and overlap counts. Compare against an activity-preserving time-shuffle baseline, exposing the assumptions and sensitivity to the chosen window. A primary coordination paper explicitly distinguishes detected coordination from whether behaviour is automated, organic, benign or malicious. ([Coordination-network methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.05658v2))

**MVP and reuse.** Review one channel's authorised distribution records, show the underlying co-actions, and let an analyst record dispositions such as routine syndication, scheduled broadcast or unclear. Include known benign alerts and volunteer campaigns. Generic uses include duplicate syndication, repetitive community submissions and internal distribution audits. An optional defence application is an aggregate, approved public-information integrity study. This is a proposed adaptation; the foreign sources do not establish this exact product's deployment. The bipartite view adds value by revealing recurrent actor–artefact combinations that a frequency table obscures, although simple duplicate counting remains the baseline.

**Competition, evaluation and assumption.** The narrow documentation gap is transparent comparison baselines and review evidence, not a missing local ability to draw coordination maps. Measure reviewer-confirmed repeated-pattern precision, false alerts on benign schedules and stability across windows. Malicious-intent accuracy is not measurable without independently established intent labels. The hardest assumption is that the chosen activity baseline represents normal behaviour; popular breaking news can otherwise appear suspicious. ([Kazee existing offering](https://kazee.id/platform/media-intelligence/id))

### 3. CoverageMap checks acknowledgements across participating organisations

**Decision and Indonesian problem.** An event, campus, franchise or volunteer consortium asks which participating organisations lack a confirmed information handoff. Indonesian disaster-communication and collaboration studies support the importance of cross-organisation participation, but do not quantify failures that this product would prevent. The product measures a defined roster, not the population reached by a public post. If the task is only counting read receipts from a single broadcaster, an ordinary table is sufficient; SNA adds value when information crosses several documented organisational relays. ([UI disaster-communication research account](https://fisip.ui.ac.id/sistem-komunikasi-krisis-dan-risiko-bencana-berbasis-media-sosial-di-indonesia/))

**Graph and minimal method.** Nodes are registered teams, organisations or community channels. Edges separately record sent, forwarded and acknowledged events, each with direction, time and evidence. A complete participant roster preserves genuine isolates. Missing acknowledgement is **unknown**, rather than proof that nobody received a message. Components and validated reachability expose broken handoffs; an acknowledgement supports a recorded response, not comprehension or future delivery. The administrative view remains available alongside the network so users can audit the underlying states.

**MVP and reuse.** Run an opt-in exercise with a small consortium, two message rounds and independently recorded acknowledgements. A synthetic exercise is acceptable if clearly labelled and excluded from claims about operational performance. Reuse the same workflow for distributed notices, outage updates and event coordination. Approved civil–military disaster-information handoffs are an optional defence scenario, not a verified foreign implementation of CoverageMap. The product requires no inference about demographic or political groups.

**Competition, evaluation and assumption.** Messaging platforms already offer receipts, and Indonesian organisational-network research exists. The narrower opportunity is typed, cross-organisation evidence with explicit unknowns; market-gap confidence is low-to-medium. Compare flags with a manual acknowledgement audit, measure false “unreached” conclusions and time to identify a broken relay. Test withheld observations separately from genuine missing delivery. The hardest assumption is that participants maintain the roster and record handoffs consistently; without that discipline, the apparent coverage gap becomes a collection artefact.

### 4. RelayResilience tests collaboration dependency

**Decision and Indonesian problem.** A consenting project consortium asks whether a validated task depends on one team or liaison and whether a backup is agreed. ITB's Cilegon study already uses organisational SNA for multi-hazard collaboration, making local methodological novelty untenable. The testable product opportunity is repeated assessment tied to accepted backup plans. Relationship structure matters because a set of good individual response times does not reveal disconnected inter-team handoffs. ([ITB institutional thesis](https://digilib.itb.ac.id/gdl/view/80997/25420011))

**Graph and minimal method.** Use teams or organisations as nodes, with consented working relationships or confirmed task-handoff edges. Preserve direction, task class and time. An opt-in survey plus liaison validation is preferable to silently treating message volume as collaboration. Compute components and directed reachability; use articulation points only on an appropriate, labelled undirected projection. A reviewer simulates unavailable teams or links and records agreed alternatives. This scenario is not proof of operational failure, and central position does not measure an employee's value or blame.

**MVP and reuse.** Start with one roster, task class and confirmed edge list. Compare the dependency diagram with a simple dependency table and conduct a small handoff exercise. Reuse spans research collaborations, customer escalations, volunteer groups, projects and incident-response teams. Non-sensitive humanitarian coordination is an optional defence demonstration. Foreign missing-data and organisational-analysis work supports the question, but no source establishes that this proposed workflow is operationally deployed. Access should be restricted to consenting participants; the public demo can aggregate to teams.

**Competition, evaluation and assumption.** Existing organisational research and collaboration tools already cover parts of the method. The bounded hypothesis is an evidence-backed review with accepted backup actions, with low-to-medium market-gap confidence. Check graph calculations against hand-worked examples, ask liaisons to validate dependencies, and measure exercise recovery time against the table-based baseline. Vary uncertain ties and disclose any conclusion that changes. This is the **preferred fallback when an organisational partner can supply reliable data**; its hardest assumption is that the reported relationships reflect the task rather than only the organisation chart.

### 5. GraphCheck tests whether an SNA conclusion survives uncertainty

**Decision and Indonesian problem.** Analysts and social-listening teams ask whether an observed ranking, community split or path is stable enough to include in a briefing. Public local product pages establish metrics but did not establish the complete evidence-and-sensitivity workflow proposed here. That does not show vendors lack it. The product hypothesis is that a reproducible review pack prevents unsupported conclusions more effectively than disconnected metric scripts. Missing-data research shows that bias depends on which nodes are missing, graph structure and the metric; random deletion alone does not model every collection bias. ([Primary missing-data study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5110009/))

**Graph and minimal method.** Import an actor-interaction graph and its provenance/coverage manifest. Keep observed, inferred and uncertain ties separate. Compare declared time windows, alias-resolution choices and a few plausible missingness scenarios, including degree-biased omissions. Show ranking overlap, component changes and community agreement on shared observed nodes. Test seeds and resolution for a selected community algorithm. The NetworkX documentation explicitly exposes these Louvain parameters, reinforcing that a partition is a parameter-dependent result, not an established social identity. ([Louvain documentation](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.community.louvain.louvain_communities.html))

**MVP and reuse.** Combine one real permitted interaction corpus with synthetic graphs whose properties are known. Include a mistaken identity merge, a missing connector and a time-window change, with visible evidence. Reuse serves researchers, vendors and agencies; an optional defence application is quality review of an unclassified assessment. This is methodological tooling, not a threat detector. Limit the first release to social-interaction graphs rather than a universal graph laboratory.

**Competition, evaluation and assumption.** Gephi, graph libraries and research scripts already supply metrics. The proposed value is reproducible evidence review and failure explanations; market-gap confidence is low. Check metric correctness on small graphs, whether planted errors are surfaced and whether reviewers draw fewer unsupported conclusions. Edge removal is a stress test under stated assumptions, not a calibrated reconstruction of the unknown complete network. The hardest assumption is commercial willingness to adopt specialist QA tooling. For the hackathon, borrow a small robustness check inside CascadeLens rather than building GraphCheck simultaneously.

## Data contracts and acceptance tests determine feasibility

The minimum common contract records **source node, target node, relation type, event time, evidence identifier, observation method and workspace**. Preserve raw events, an alias-review table and a coverage manifest separately from aggregated weights. Stable platform IDs take precedence over similar names; cross-platform identity merging requires independent evidence. Declare directedness, weighting and temporal boundaries before calculating metrics. In weighted betweenness, NetworkX treats weights as distances, so passing message counts directly can reverse the intended interpretation. PageRank depends on link orientation. These are implementation details with material consequences, not reasons to add more algorithms. ([Betweenness](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.centrality.betweenness_centrality.html), [PageRank](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.link_analysis.pagerank_alg.pagerank.html))

Data access is the first gate. Drone Emprit says its free Academic service stopped in 2023 after Twitter access changed. A historical scraping example therefore does not establish current free access. Owner-authorised CSVs provide a repeatable offline path; Bluesky's official public read endpoints offer an optional connector, with evolving limits and no guarantee of a complete diffusion graph. Platform visibility does not settle collection, training or redistribution rights. ([Provider notice](https://pers.droneemprit.id/layanan-drone-emprit-academic-berhenti/), [Bluesky API](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/api/app-bsky-feed-get-author-feed), [rate limits](https://bsky.network/docs/rate-limits/))

Available datasets support different tests. SNAP's email-Eu-core-temporal provides directed, anonymised email events but excludes external messages. Its Reddit Hyperlink Network connects communities through dated hyperlinks, not individual exposure chains. Dataset rights need checking separately from SNAP's software licence. SocioPatterns' workplace metadata explicitly lists CC0 and describes anonymous face-to-face contacts; this is a clearer licensed contact-network demonstration, but proximity does not establish task collaboration or online diffusion. A shared software schema can process these relations while displaying their distinct meanings. ([Email dataset](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/email-Eu-core-temporal.html), [Reddit network](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/soc-RedditHyperlinks.html), [SocioPatterns metadata](https://github.com/SocioPatterns/sociopatterns.github.io/blob/main/_datasets/contacts-in-a-workplace.md))

For CascadeLens, implement **import, validate, replay, inspect evidence, record correction and export a bounded finding**. SQLite/PostgreSQL, a familiar Python graph library and one renderer are sufficient for a small replay. No graph database, GNN or LLM is inherently required. Prove reuse on two appropriate interaction datasets with the same pipeline; a contact graph can test generic edge handling but cannot validate an online transmission claim. Benchmark actual hardware before claiming real-time performance or scale.

Acceptance should combine sampled edge fidelity, temporal consistency, hand-calculated graph checks and one reviewer task compared with a table. Include duplicate ingestion, reversed edges, root-only reposts, ambiguous aliases, deleted observations and a static path impossible in time order. For learned components, hold out later periods or events and prevent duplicate/thread leakage. Report unsupported conclusions alongside task accuracy and time. Centrality describes position in the observed graph; communities describe an algorithmic partition. Neither establishes credibility, causal influence, belief or hostile intent.

The next investment should secure an edge-rich permitted dataset and replay one real analyst decision. If that access fails, RelayResilience offers a cleaner route with a consenting partner. Demonstrable reuse means changing data and declared relation semantics while preserving the workflow; a list of hypothetical sectors supplies weaker evidence. That boundary gives the project a concrete hackathon task and a product that can serve later customers.

**Not checked:** private systems, authenticated vendor demonstrations, user interviews, live API execution, software benchmarks or complete licence/IP review. Equivalent capabilities may already exist; all five product gaps and performance benefits remain hypotheses pending validation.
