# Build TemuRiset on a reusable core

**TemuRiset should become a reusable invention-partnership product core, initially implemented as a modular monolith.** Its common job is helping inventors find investors or other supporters, exchange appropriate evidence, evaluate fit and agree on next steps. White-label branding is one possible offering; it does not describe all the functional reuse the product needs. SaaS delivery and multitenancy are separate choices about operating and isolating a running service. The proposed base should retain strong transaction, confidentiality and consent rules while making sector checklists, organizational review and external integrations deliberate variations. This recommendation applies product-family principles without requiring a large framework, a plugin marketplace or microservices. It is a design proposal based on primary sources, not a claim that a reusable TemuRiset implementation or validated business model already exists.

Research date: **31 October 2026, Asia/Jakarta**. The available baseline was TemuRiset PRD v1.0 dated 20 October 2026, attached to the referenced chat. No TemuRiset PRD filename was found in the current checkout or the three inspected remote heads. The new generic specification therefore documents an available-source generalization rather than claiming to revise a newer repository PRD. All external pages cited below were opened during the supporting research; product pages establish described capabilities, not independently demonstrated outcomes.

## Inventor support is broader than research commercialization

The original product already had useful generic behavior: portfolios, published needs, two-sided matching, evidence checks, pitching, revisions, scheduling and relationship confirmation. Its remaining bias was in the people, examples, review gates, health-sector evidence and demonstration criteria that surrounded those behaviors. Replacing a university name with a configurable logo would leave those assumptions intact. The more meaningful change is to make an independent person, startup, corporate team, nonprofit or community group equally valid participants, then attach additional requirements to the actual opportunity rather than to the existence of an account.

WIPO GREEN organizes its marketplace around technology and service providers and seekers, including collaboration, licensing and distribution contexts. Its green-technology scope does not prove a universal marketplace will succeed, but its provider-and-need model is a useful counterexample to treating institutional research as the only source of inventions. Halo likewise distinguishes several contributor types, including researchers, startup representatives and suppliers/corporations. Its own institutional-verification choices should not be copied as universal prerequisites. **The inference for TemuRiset is an invention-centered catalog with flexible managing parties**, rather than a researcher directory with investors added later. ([WIPO GREEN](https://www3.wipo.int/wipogreen/en/aboutus/); [Halo innovator guide](https://knowledge.halo.science/articles/8946411648-guide-for-innovators)).

Investor discovery deserves a first-class path. Relevant recipient data includes sectors, accepted stages, geography, support forms, financial contribution ranges, current intake and preferred application route. OpenVC's published investor database and outreach pages expose these kinds of criteria and routes. They support the design need for explicit mandates; they do not establish that directory presence means an investor is currently interested, that sending a deck means it was accepted, or that an automated fit score predicts funding. TemuRiset should show the distinction between a general thesis, active intake and an external contact route. ([OpenVC investor database](https://www.openvc.app/investor-database); [OpenVC outreach](https://www.openvc.app/investor-outreach)).

One important domain correction is to separate **company fundraising from invention-specific project support**. A company can raise once to advance several inventions. The target raise, an individual investor's ticket, a pilot budget, a licensing fee and an equity percentage are different quantities. The generic model therefore introduces a company/venture FundingRequest, optionally linked to multiple invention versions, alongside invention SupportRequests and ProjectBudgets. This schema is our proposed invariant, not an external platform's prescribed model. It prevents one raise being presented as several raises and prevents a small investor ticket being incorrectly compared against the entire round target.

A pre-incorporation team can still seek discussion when a recipient accepts that stage. The product must label a proposed venture honestly and require an actual identified entity before recording a confirmed equity relationship. That distinction preserves early-stage access without fabricating a company. Company financing stage is also separate from an invention's technical maturity. A company with revenue can be developing a new prototype, while an individual can have a mature invention without a registered business.

Ownership needs similar precision. WIPO explains that creator and owner can differ depending on employment, policy, funding, agreements and applicable law; disclosure before filing can also affect protection. TemuRiset cannot infer rights from authorship or a logo. The base therefore separates creator, managing party, asserted rights holder and authority to act, with sources and review scope. This is a product requirement for truthful representation and deliberate disclosure, not a legal conclusion about any submitted invention. ([WIPO technology-transfer FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/faq)).

## Product-family reuse describes the goal more accurately

SEI describes software product lines through related systems, managed common assets and a defined production approach. The useful principle here is maintained commonality: shared fixes, contracts and operating practices remain part of the family rather than being copied once and forgotten. TemuRiset fits a lightweight interpretation when future clients still need invention discovery, partner evaluation, controlled disclosure and collaboration. That does not imply a mature product-line engineering program has already been built. ([SEI software product lines curriculum](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/software-product-lines-curriculum/)).

White-label describes an organization presenting a supplier's product under its own brand. Logo, domain, colors and approved wording are branding variations, but a technical review gate or new source integration changes behavior. Those differences need capabilities and adapters, not just a theme. SaaS describes hosted service delivery, whereas multitenancy describes architectural sharing between customer boundaries. They can coexist with white-label presentation or with a common product family, but none follows automatically from using one repository. ([Shopify white-label guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/white-label); [Microsoft SaaS and multitenancy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/guide/saas-multitenant-solution-architecture/)).

**The proposed contract is a shared base PRD plus a client addendum.** The base fixes domain meanings and safety-critical invariants. The addendum records actual users, required outcomes, enabled capabilities, configuration, integrations, information-sharing boundaries and client-specific acceptance. A client asking for an additional evidence checklist should supply its source, scope, responsible reviewer and applicable stage. That checklist must not silently become mandatory for every inventor in every deployment.

This approach also defines when reuse should stop. An incubator, invention community or corporate innovation program can share the same jobs while varying its taxonomy, intake and review. An unrelated operational system can borrow proven authentication, storage or UI patterns without inheriting the invention marketplace domain. Reusing HematDaya's modularity and release discipline is sensible; importing its electricity-accounting model would be a category error. A solid base is bounded enough that a team can explain what does not belong inside it.

Templates and forks solve different maintenance problems. GitHub templates generate a new project with an independent initial history; they accelerate setup but do not provide a continuing dependency that receives upstream fixes. Forks retain lineage, but divergent client changes still require merging and backport decisions. For related TemuRiset variants, keep a maintained common source and explicit extension points. If an independent product or deliberate fork is necessary, assign upgrade and security-maintenance ownership instead of promising automatic propagation. ([GitHub repository templates](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/creating-and-managing-repositories/creating-a-repository-from-a-template)).

## A modular monolith gives boundaries without premature services

The proposed starting architecture is one backend application with internal modules, a responsive web client, PostgreSQL, private object storage and an optional worker from the same release. Portfolio/evidence, support/funding, partner intake, matching, pitch collaboration, scheduling and relationship records each own their rules. Identity/access and delivery/operations provide tightly scoped shared capabilities. Module interfaces expose authorized use cases and projections; callers do not bypass them by directly reading another module's repository.

Simon Brown's modular-monolith discussion makes the important point that deployment and modularity are different dimensions. A folder hierarchy alone does not prevent a component from depending on another's internals. The inference for this product is to enforce dependency direction and repository ownership through visibility or architecture checks, while letting a single database transaction protect submission, booking and confirmation use cases. The UI, importer and worker all call the same domain authority. ([Simon Brown, Modular monolith](https://simonbrown.je/modular-monolith/)).

Fowler's monolith-first advice highlights the operational premium of services and the difficulty of finding stable boundaries early. He also describes that evidence as anecdotal and tentative. A modular monolith is therefore a practical small-team starting choice, not a promise that later service extraction will be easy. Extract a service only when independent ownership, measured contention, release cadence or an actual isolation requirement justifies the added operating cost. ([Fowler, Monolith First](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/MonolithFirst.html)).

Variation should be classified before implementation. **Configuration** handles approved content, branding, locale and named policy selections. A **capability** implements a repeatable business difference with prerequisites, tests and explicit disabled behavior. An **adapter** handles a concrete external boundary such as a catalog, identity provider, storage service, model runtime or calendar. This is a proposed TemuRiset design, not a requirement to implement every hypothetical provider. It avoids two costly extremes: scattered client-name conditionals and an abstract plugin platform that has no actual consumers.

The composition file stays small, validated and versioned. It names the source release, schema/config revision, enabled capability versions, approved checklist policies and adapter versions; credentials remain in secret storage. Invalid combinations fail before traffic is served. Disabling AI leaves manual facts and template briefs usable. Disabling an external calendar leaves internal conflict checks and ICS. Disabling company fundraising preserves the project-support journey and requires an explicit treatment of historical records. A pending review queue cannot be abandoned by switching off its capability. Microsoft's guidance supports a common codebase and consistent deployment while distinguishing persistent product differences from temporary rollout flags. ([Microsoft deployment and configuration approaches](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/guide/multitenant/approaches/deployment-configuration)).

The first useful proof is a complete flow plus one justified variation. It is not the number of modules, packages or feature flags. The new PRD specifies personal inventor and investor contexts, optional organization review and a project-only composition to test that both enabled and disabled paths remain meaningful. A future client then changes a documented composition or adds one justified capability instead of cloning the application.

## Reliable matching must preserve evidence and human intent

The best ideas in the baseline concern distinctions that remain valuable across sectors: current versus planned capability, evidence present versus reviewed, eligibility versus ranking, discussion approval versus relationship, and proposal versus booking. The generic base keeps those distinctions and makes investment semantics equally explicit. Inpart's campaigns illustrate opportunity-specific requirements, review and later collaboration outcomes; Halo's application guide describes finding opportunities, saving progress and tracking proposals. These are useful workflow patterns, not proof that every user needs a large dashboard or that an introduction produces an agreement. ([Inpart Campaigns](https://www.inpart.io/network/campaigns); [Halo opportunity guide](https://knowledge.halo.science/articles/4031823346-finding-and-applying-to-opportunities-on-halo)).

Matching should begin with explicit needs, available support and typed criteria. Known failed mandatory rules cannot be canceled by a high relevance score. Missing or insufficiently reviewed evidence remains unknown. For a small catalog, evaluating all permitted pairs with lexical relevance and deterministic explanations is enough to establish a baseline. Hybrid retrieval and reranking are optional measured improvements. RRF has a documented rank-fusion basis, and retrieve-and-rerank is an established implementation pattern; neither validates a particular cross-sector model or TemuRiset score threshold. The proposed reciprocal score therefore remains a versioned review-priority index with missing-information bounds, never a probability of investment. ([Original RRF paper](https://cormack.uwaterloo.ca/cormacksigir09-rrf.pdf); [Sentence Transformers retrieval and reranking](https://sbert.net/examples/sentence_transformer/applications/retrieve_rerank/README.html)).

Confidentiality must follow data through search, cache, background jobs, model input, generated briefs and exports. OWASP's authorization guidance calls for deny-by-default checks on every request, while its multitenancy guidance extends verified context and isolation to caches, files and asynchronous consumers. The proposed product consequence is an explicitly public catalog projection and named grants for private versions. A historical submission records what happened, but cannot grant perpetual access to revoked files or confidential derived text. Prior downloads cannot be technically recalled; the interface needs to say so. ([OWASP authorization](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html); [OWASP multi-tenant security](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Multi_Tenant_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html)).

Human intent also requires transaction protection. Idempotency keys prevent retried submissions from becoming duplicates; expected versions prevent stale decisions. An outbox commits notification intent with the state change, while delivery retries independently. Meeting acceptance must reserve all required participants atomically; database range exclusion is a concrete way to prevent overlapping reservations. Those mechanisms can be implemented in one application without adopting a distributed-service architecture. ([AWS idempotent APIs](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/); [AWS transactional outbox](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/transactional-outbox.html); [PostgreSQL range types](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/rangetypes.html)).

Relationship confirmation remains separate from legal execution. WIPO distinguishes licensing, assignment, collaboration and confidentiality agreements, each requiring contextual scope. TemuRiset records a summary confirmed by both authorized parties; it must not describe that record as an executed license, independent legal review or share issuance. Both confirm the same summary/disclosure-ceiling version, with public fields limited to the intersection of their independently selected consent and any later restrictions. Project finance must never imply equity ownership. ([WIPO technology-transfer agreements](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/agreements)).

## Shared releases need explicit deployment and learning plans

An organization, workspace, marketplace, customer, tenant and deployment are different concepts. The initial TemuRiset deployment should intentionally permit published discovery and granted collaboration across personal and organization contexts. Treating every organization as a sealed tenant would obstruct the product's core job; treating every record as global would expose private work. An isolated client deployment can use the same source release with its own storage, database, secrets and identity system. It shares code, not users, permissions or network reach. Cross-deployment discovery or pooled SaaS needs a new explicit design and operating decision.

Release reuse is a maintenance process. Keep source/configuration/adapter/schema inventories, run shared invariants and every supported composition, create reproducible artifacts, exercise migration and restore, then roll out deliberately per environment. A new source tag does not prove a client's running system has changed. Use expand/migrate/contract database changes and document when code rollback is insufficient and forward repair or backup restore is needed. If a future independently released consumer needs a package, define its public contract and upgrade process then; internal modules do not each need independent release machinery.

The generic acceptance set deliberately crosses five sectors and includes an independent inventor without a company, uncertain rights, planned functionality, a nonprofit early concept and one company raise linked to two inventions. These are fictional regression contracts, not a market-size claim. Evaluate matching on labels grouped by invention, company/request and related opportunities, keeping paraphrases together. Tuning on held-out cases would give misleading results; established leakage guidance supports separating model selection and final evaluation. ([scikit-learn common pitfalls](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html)).

The unresolved questions are commercial and operational as much as technical: which initial cohort has real complementary demand, who verifies active intake, who reviews evidence, which markets/currencies are initially supported and who maintains each deployment. Neither this research nor the synthetic fixtures answers those questions. No benchmark, interview study, penetration test, accessibility assessment or funding outcome has been run. The base is solid as a specification when it makes those limits visible and provides exact tests, rather than converting assumptions into claims.

## Conclusion

The most valuable shared asset is the meaning of a collaboration: who asks, who can help, what is known, what is shared and what both parties have actually agreed. Preserving that meaning makes new branding and sector-specific gates easier to add without creating several incompatible products. The next evidence should come from implementing the smallest complete flow and one real client variation, then revising the boundaries where that work exposes friction.

The resulting repository package contains the authoritative generic PRD, a source-audited summary and a client-extension contract. It is ready to guide design and implementation, with all runtime acceptance scenarios still marked not run. The boundary between a reusable core and a speculative platform is maintained by requiring a concrete user need, a tested variation and an accountable release owner for every extension.
