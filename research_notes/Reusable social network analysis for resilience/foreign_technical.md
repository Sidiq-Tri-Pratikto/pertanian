# Foreign SNA precedents and reusable technical foundations

Research cutoff: 26 October 2026, Asia/Jakarta. Public evidence supports several defence applications of network analysis, but mixes research, trials, published capability descriptions and methodological studies. No claim of absence in Indonesia is justified by these sources.

## Which foreign defence examples are publicly documented?

### Takeaway
The most reusable lessons concern reconstructing observed information-sharing relationships, understanding communication bottlenecks, and checking how incomplete data changes a conclusion. Four strong primary cases below are sufficient; two additional examples have access limitations and should be qualified rather than promoted as deployed systems.

### Cited Findings
- **Australia/DSTG and US Army researchers: RAPID — developed platform and partner-reported trials.** Melbourne's 30 January 2023 case study describes a platform developed with DSTG, modelling interactions among users, posts and topics; it says the platform was trialled by DSTG and US Army Research Laboratory researchers. The page describes a 2018 test and event comparisons/time sequencing. This is evidence of development and trials, not independent proof of the accuracy of account authenticity judgments or permanent operational deployment. **Social meaning:** people/accounts, posts and topics as entities; observed discussion, authorship, follower and other interaction links. Borrow evidence filtering, explicit relations and event comparison, not unverified claims that graph structure identifies malicious people. — [University of Melbourne primary partner case study](https://research.unimelb.edu.au/partnerships/case-studies/ai-platform-targets-crowd-sourced-data-for-military-intelligence)
- **United States/DARPA: SocialSim — completed research programme.** The official programme describes research on the spread and evolution of online information, explicitly naming disaster-relief information delivery among possible applications. The page marks it complete and no longer maintained. It does not establish a deployed diffusion predictor or forecast accuracy. **Social meaning:** interactions among online participants and information objects over time; simulation of these interactions is a separate task from displaying an observed network. Its strongest lesson is dual civilian/defence relevance, rather than a reason to implement speculative behavioural simulation in the MVP. — [DARPA official programme](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/computational-simulation-of-online-social-behavior)
- **NATO StratCom COE: AI in Support of StratCom Capabilities, January 2024 — published research/methodology.** Its network-analysis section discusses accounts, hashtags and domains, retweet/co-hashtag networks, density and centrality; it illustrates retweet communities with Louvain and k-core analysis. The report explicitly states that its views are not NATO policy. It is evidence of a technical study, not a NATO-wide operational product. The transferable elements are typed networks, community structure and transparent parameters; the report's broader targeting/persuasion ambitions are unnecessary for our product. — [Primary report, network-analysis section printed p.24](https://stratcomcoe.org/publications/download/Revised-AI-in-Support-of-StratCom-Capabilities-DIGITAL---Copy.pdf)
- **Canada/Defence R&D Canada: applicability study, December 2012 — methodological research for a proof-of-concept.** The government catalogue's abstract says the study assessed theoretical SNA methods against computational, flow/topological and missing-data requirements and warned that conventional analyses do not automatically satisfy the military context. Its emphasis on missing-data robustness is useful to any incomplete customer, organisation or online network. This is a historical technical study, not proof of a current deployed Canadian platform; no operational counterinsurgency methods need be copied. — [Canadian government primary catalogue and abstract](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.876621/publication.html)
- **United States Army: OEVis — official capability description, limited verification.** An indexed official Army page describes a network-analysis platform for social/communication relationships, centrality and information flows. Direct fetch timed out twice, so confidence is restricted to the indexed primary text; neither actual user deployment nor performance was independently verified. Suitable as a short additional example only, labelled **officially described capability**, not a proven production success. — [US Army official page](https://oe.t2com.army.mil/oe-vis-operational-environment-visualization/)
- **UK Dstl/QinetiQ in JFCOM Multinational Experiment 4, 2006 — experiment analysis, supplementary.** The indexed primary conference-paper abstract describes applying SNA to organisational robustness in an experimental multinational headquarters. Full-text retrieval failed. It supports a consented organisational-collaboration/resilience analogue at a high level, not present-day deployment or tactical decision support. — [Original conference paper](https://www.dodccrp.org/events/11th_ICCRTS/html/papers/138.pdf)

### Inferences
- A generic **evidence-backed temporal network workbench** can be useful across public information, crisis coordination, customer/community conversations and authorised organisational collaboration. Defence is a possible adopter, not a required hard-coded data model.
- The strongest differentiation hypothesis is showing **which observations create an edge, what time and coverage bounds apply, and whether conclusions survive plausible data loss**. More centrality plots alone are weak differentiation.
- Prefer an information-sharing replay or an anonymised collaboration exercise as a defence demonstration. Neither requires inferring individual ideology, loyalty, psychological traits or hostile intent.
- Defence research validates relevance, not willingness to buy, Indonesian novelty, or competitive superiority. The separate Indonesian researcher has found explicit TNI use of SNA; do not suggest basic defence SNA is absent locally.

### Gaps
- No authenticated systems, military data, procurement awards, performance benchmarks or active deployment accounts were inspected.
- Canada's proposed 2026 COTS SNA procurement was found through tender aggregators, but the primary CanadaBuys notice was not successfully verified. Exclude it as deployment proof. Its advertised project interdependencies may also be an adjacent dependency graph rather than social relationships.
- UK Dstl's 2016 online-audiences page establishes research exists, but its landing page alone does not establish a particular SNA technique or implemented product. — [Primary landing page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/understanding-online-audiences)

## What graph semantics and algorithms are appropriate for a reusable MVP?

### Takeaway
Define the relation before computing a score. An observed reply, a shared URL, a follower connection, a department contact and a hyperlink between communities are different kinds of evidence; collapsing them into one undifferentiated graph can change the meaning of every result.

### Cited Findings
- NetworkX defines betweenness through shortest paths. Its weighted implementation treats weights as **distances**, not interaction strengths; approximation and direction affect the calculation. Merely passing a high message count as a distance can invert the intended interpretation. — [Official betweenness documentation](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.centrality.betweenness_centrality.html)
- NetworkX describes PageRank as ranking nodes from incoming links; it converts an undirected graph into reciprocal directed edges. Thus edge orientation is part of the analytical specification. — [Official PageRank documentation](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.link_analysis.pagerank_alg.pagerank.html)
- Louvain is a modularity-optimisation heuristic; resolution changes community size and the implementation exposes a random seed. Self-loop treatment matters. An algorithmic partition is not a validated real-world group identity. — [Official Louvain documentation](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.community.louvain.louvain_communities.html)
- Pacheco et al.'s primary coordination paper builds networks from unexpectedly similar behavioural traces, and explicitly describes coordination regardless of automated/organic nature or malicious/benign intent. A similarity edge does not establish who controls the accounts, criminality, or a false claim. — [ICWSM 2021 author paper, introduction and methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.05658v2)
- Smith, Moody and Morgan's primary missing-data study finds that bias depends on which nodes are missing, graph structure and the metric. Random deletion is not a complete model of real API/collection bias. Missing data need not invalidate all analyses, but measurement-specific robustness must be checked. — [Social Networks 2017 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5110009/)
- Temporal-network research treats the order and timing of interactions as part of the system, rather than a cosmetic animation over a static graph. — [Holme and Saramäki, Temporal Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1780)

### Inferences
Minimum reusable schema and interpretation rules, proposed rather than tested:
1. An observation keeps `source_id`, `target_id`, `relation_type`, `event_time`, `evidence_id`/URL, workspace/platform identifier and ingestion time. Preserve stable platform IDs; retain a separate, reviewable alias table. Similar handles alone cannot justify cross-platform identity merging.
2. For a reply graph, declare sender → recipient. For sharing graphs, choose and disclose direction, such as source author → observed resharer. A platform may link every reshare to the original post; that produces an observed star, not proof of the actual person-to-person transmission chain.
3. Preserve direct and inferred edges separately. Shared-link similarity can connect two accounts without any observed interaction; label it a similarity relation, not a social tie or endorsement. A community hyperlink indicates linking, not necessarily support or audience exposure.
4. Keep edge types/layers separate. Aggregate repeated events only within a declared time window and document the weight rule. Keep original events available so the analyst can inspect why an edge exists.
5. Begin with components, degree/in-degree/out-degree, one documented community method and a timeline. Add betweenness or PageRank only when their structural question serves the user decision. Centrality describes graph position under the observed boundary; it does not measure credibility, causal influence, correctness or intent.
6. Label the oldest item as **first observed in this corpus**, not the true origin. A time-respecting path requires interactions in a possible temporal order; even such a path does not prove causal exposure or persuasion.
7. For a coordination-pattern option, show shared evidence and threshold sensitivity in an analyst queue. Benign broadcast schedules, common breaking news and volunteer campaigns must be represented in the false-positive tests. No automatic malicious-account label follows from coordination.
8. For small demo data, a familiar Python graph library and one interactive renderer suffice; benchmark actual response times before claiming real-time scale. A graph database, GNN and streaming infrastructure are optional later needs, not prerequisites for a useful MVP.

### Gaps
- No algorithm, graph runtime, prediction model, API or interface was executed. Do not quote hypothetical accuracy, speedups or node limits as achieved performance.
- A similarity threshold, community resolution and collection window cannot be selected universally from this literature. They depend on the relation and task, and require validation.
- Edge deletion sensitivity is a stress test under stated assumptions, not a statistically calibrated confidence interval or a reconstruction of the unknown complete network.

## Which data and evaluation plans make the prototype credible?

### Takeaway
Choose an edge-rich dataset first. An ordinary collection of posts without replies, reshare references, affiliations or other documented relations cannot support a faithful social network just by adding a graph layout. A time-stamped authorised CSV is sufficient to demonstrate a reusable workflow.

### Cited Findings
- **SNAP email-Eu-core-temporal:** anonymised sender/recipient email events inside a European research institution. It explicitly excludes messages to/from the outside world and supplies directed `(u,v,t)` events; IDs in department extracts differ from IDs in the full data. Useful for a collaboration/time-window test, not evidence of response quality, organisational hierarchy or the complete real network. Dataset-specific reuse terms still need inspection; SNAP software's BSD licence is not a blanket licence for every dataset. — [Dataset and format](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/email-Eu-core-temporal.html), [SNAP software licence](https://snap.stanford.edu/snap/download.html)
- **SNAP Reddit Hyperlink Network:** communities are nodes and hyperlinks between posts in different communities are directed, signed, temporal edges. The 2014–2017 archive supports historical community-connection experiments. Its sentiment signs are dataset annotations, not present-day truth/hostility labels. A fresh Reddit dataset would have different access and rights requirements. — [Dataset](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/soc-RedditHyperlinks.html)
- **SocioPatterns workplace contacts:** publisher-controlled repository describes anonymous, time-stamped face-to-face contacts in a French office and department metadata. It explicitly lists CC0 public-domain dedication and requests the underlying paper be cited. This is the clearest inspected licence for a small reusable collaboration/contact demonstration; physical contact is not evidence of task communication. — [Original publisher dataset metadata](https://github.com/SocioPatterns/sociopatterns.github.io/blob/main/_datasets/contacts-in-a-workplace.md)
- **Bluesky:** official HTTP documentation describes public AppView read endpoints, including posts, threads, quotes and repost-related endpoints. Its rate-limit documentation says services vary and limits evolve. This is a possible optional live connector, not a guaranteed complete or representative information-diffusion graph; collection and reuse rights require separate review. No endpoint was called in this research. — [Official API reference](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/api/app-bsky-feed-get-author-feed), [Official rate-limit guidance](https://bsky.network/docs/rate-limits/)
- SNAP's collection separately identifies social, communication, collaboration, temporal and other graph types. It should be used to select a relation matching the product, rather than treating citation, infrastructure or co-purchase networks as equivalent social interactions. — [Dataset catalogue](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/)

### Inferences
Suggested evaluation, all **proposed**, with no achieved metrics:
- **Edge fidelity:** manually audit a sample against original event evidence; measure source/target identity, direction, timestamp and relation accuracy separately. Test alias mistakes, deleted posts, self-loops and duplicate ingestion. A synthetic graph with known edges is a useful deterministic self-check, not proof of field accuracy.
- **Time validity:** include a case where a static path exists but the edge order makes the proposed diffusion path impossible. Verify no future edge is used to explain an earlier event.
- **Community and ranking stability:** repeat across declared seeds/resolutions and plausible random plus degree-biased missingness. Report rank overlap/correlation, component changes and partition agreement on common observed nodes. Do not call every detected community a real social group.
- **Ground truth where available:** evaluate planted synthetic communities or a documented benchmark grouping, while acknowledging that department labels need not equal interaction communities. Use reviewer-confirmed relations for real-world edge tests.
- **Temporal separation:** hold out later periods or whole events for any learned classification; keep duplicate posts, related threads and identities from leaking across the intended evaluation boundary.
- **User benefit:** compare a reviewer performing one concrete task with a chronological table versus the evidence-linked network: reconstruct observed sharing, identify a documented handoff bottleneck, or locate a missing provenance link. Measure task accuracy/time and unsupported conclusions, rather than claiming that attractive graph layouts establish usefulness.
- **Reuse demonstration:** run the same observation schema, filtering and evidence inspection on two appropriate datasets, such as an observed public sharing replay and anonymised organisational communication. Changing labels/data should suffice; a separate app per sector defeats the stated goal.
- **Data-access fallback:** owner-authorised exports or a licensed historical dataset should make the demo repeatable offline. Do not make unrestricted X/Instagram follower harvesting, private messaging or cross-platform identity resolution a hidden requirement.

### Gaps
- No dataset files were downloaded or licence conditions exhaustively reviewed. The SocioPatterns explicit CC0 statement is stronger than assumptions about the SNAP archive, but attribution and context still matter.
- No representative Indonesian social graph is supplied by these foreign archives. A synthetic Indonesian-labelled graph is only a simulation and must be labelled accordingly.
- No user interviews, competitor trials, live connectors, model training or operational defence access were performed. Public research cannot prove absence from Indonesian commercial or internal systems.
