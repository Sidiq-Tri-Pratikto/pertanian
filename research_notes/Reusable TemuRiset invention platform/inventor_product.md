# Generic inventor and investor product requirements

Research scope: a reusable invention-discovery and collaboration product for individuals, teams, companies and other organizations. Sources were opened on **31 October 2026**. Product descriptions establish advertised workflows, not independently measured effectiveness. The existing Indonesian PRD is useful input, but its institution, competition and health-sector examples should not define the generic product.

## What domain and personas make the product portable across inventors and sectors?

### Takeaway

Use an **invention or innovation project** as the discoverable asset, with people, managing teams, rights holders and legal entities modeled separately. Preserve two simple onboarding intentions—**Inventor** and **Investor or Partner**—without assuming that every inventor works at a university or owns a company.

### Cited Findings

- WIPO GREEN describes a marketplace connecting technology and service providers with seekers of innovative solutions. Its network includes technology owners and individuals or companies interested in commercialization, licensing or distribution. This is a useful example of a domain organized around offers and needs rather than one type of institution. Its subject matter is green technology; it does not validate a universal invention marketplace. — [WIPO GREEN, About](https://www3.wipo.int/wipogreen/en/aboutus/)
- Halo's innovator onboarding explicitly distinguishes researchers, startup or spinout representatives, and suppliers or corporations. Profiles select disciplines and expertise, which influence opportunity recommendations and can be edited. Its instructions request institutional verification in its own context; that is a product choice rather than evidence that generic inventors require institutional affiliation. — [Halo, Guide for Innovators](https://knowledge.halo.science/articles/8946411648-guide-for-innovators)
- WIPO explains that creators and owners need not be the same party. Ownership can depend on the type of work, creator's relationship, resources or funding, applicable law, organizational policy and agreements. It also warns that public disclosure before patent filing can affect protection, with rules differing by jurisdiction. The product therefore cannot infer rights or safe disclosure merely from authorship, an organization logo, an uploaded file or a generic checkbox. These observations are not a jurisdiction-specific legal determination. — [WIPO, Technology Transfer FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/faq)

### Inferences

- The generic asset should cover a physical device, software product, process, material, formulation or other applied solution. “Invention” is a plain product label, not a declaration of patentability, novelty or exclusive legal rights. A project must explain a problem, intended users, current solution, stage and desired support. Sector-specific evidence belongs in optional, versioned requirement packs rather than mandatory health, university or laboratory fields. This is a proposed TemuRiset domain boundary.
- An independent maker, startup founder, corporate R&D employee, nonprofit team, community team or researcher should be able to begin with the same short onboarding. Affiliation, company incorporation and patent number should not be universal account requirements. Their relevance is established by a particular opportunity. This extends the multiple contributor categories observed in [Halo](https://knowledge.halo.science/articles/8946411648-guide-for-innovators), rather than copying its institution-first verification flow.
- Represent `Person`, `Organization`, `Membership`, `Invention`, `Contribution`, `ManagingParty`, `RightsClaim` and `LegalEntity` as different concepts. Each rights claim needs its source, asserted scope, status, reviewer when applicable and effective dates. Editing permission, inventorship, ownership, authority to pitch, authority to license and authority to confirm an agreement must remain distinguishable. This design follows the ownership ambiguity documented by [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/faq); it is not a legal ownership engine.
- Use one initial onboarding intention, then scoped workspace capabilities. A user acting for a manufacturer might seek inventions in one workspace and manage its own invention in another. Do not automatically combine permissions. An optional reviewer role should be available to any organization that needs publication or submission approval, not only universities.
- Investor must be a first-class subtype within the partner side. Other subtypes can offer development, validation, manufacturing, licensing, distribution, mentoring or facilities. Hide unrelated fields until the user selects a support form. Keeping these within one partner framework prevents a proliferation of onboarding personas while preserving meaningful differences.

### Gaps

- No user interviews establish which generic label—Inventor, Innovator or Founder—works best across these groups. Test label comprehension with independent inventors and investors before finalizing UI copy.
- This research does not establish that all sectors should launch simultaneously. A generic schema can support multiple sectors while the first release deliberately recruits a narrow, well-understood supply-and-demand cohort.
- No universal rule can settle whether a submitted idea is sufficiently original, commercially valuable or protectable. Those assessments require evidence and, where necessary, qualified review.

## How should investment discovery coexist with project support, licensing and published needs?

### Takeaway

Match an explicit **support request** to a partner's active **mandate or opportunity**. Separate a company fundraising request from an invention-specific project budget; funding a project, licensing an asset and investing in company equity are different relationships.

### Cited Findings

- OpenVC's canonical investor database displays geography, check ranges, stages and industries. Its example stages include idea or patent, prototype, early revenue and scaling. These fields show why investor fit needs structured mandate information beyond shared sector keywords. The page is vendor material, and its advertised database size or accuracy has not been independently audited here. The working canonical URL is `www.openvc.app`; the search-result `blog.openvc.app/investor-database` returned 404. — [OpenVC, Investor Database](https://www.openvc.app/investor-database)
- OpenVC's outreach page describes different accepted routes: submitting a deck, sending a reviewed email, using an external form, or finding an introduction. It connects search, a shortlist or CRM, personalized outreach and conversation tracking. This supports storing the recipient's preferred contact route. It does not establish that TemuRiset should automate email or promise a response. The vendor's response and fundraising outcome statistics are deliberately not used as acceptance targets. — [OpenVC, Investor Outreach](https://www.openvc.app/investor-outreach)
- Inpart Campaigns differentiates opportunities to commercialize existing assets from research proposals addressing a client's challenge. It describes requirements, submissions, review, consent, introductions and possible outcomes including research funding, resource sharing, licensing and strategic partnerships. Eligibility can differ by campaign. Subsequent collaboration terms are negotiated by the parties. This is evidence for opportunity-specific eligibility and relationship types, not proof that all collaborators need the same application or that an introduction guarantees an agreement. — [Inpart, Connect Campaigns](https://www.inpart.io/network/campaigns)

### Inferences

- Model `SupportRequest` with requester, linked invention(s), desired outcome, relationship form, support categories, proposed scope, stage, geography, timing and optional financial ask. Model the recipient's `Mandate` or `Opportunity` with corresponding criteria, offered resources, intake status, contact route and review expectations. A partner may publish several opportunities; never combine requirements from one with resources promised in another.
- Add a distinct `FundingRequest` for company-level fundraising. Store target raise, currency, intended use, company stage, proposed financing form and linked inventions. An individual check size is not the same as the total round target. One raise linked to three inventions must not become three separate raises or three ownership stakes. These distinctions are proposed domain invariants for TemuRiset; OpenVC's check-range and stage fields motivate explicit investor criteria but do not prescribe this schema. — [OpenVC](https://www.openvc.app/investor-database)
- Keep project funding, equity, licensing and nonfinancial support as explicit forms. A monetary project contribution does not imply company shares or transfer of IP. License scope can involve permitted use, territory and duration; detailed legal terms remain agreed outside the app or in a later, explicitly designed agreement feature. The base product records declared relationship summaries and consent rather than issuing rights.
- Allow a pre-incorporation team to discover and pitch an investor who accepts that stage. Show incorporation as a milestone or criterion when relevant. Do not claim an actual equity relationship in a nonexistent company: a confirmed equity record needs an identified legal entity. “No company yet” and “will form a company” are clear data states, not fabricated company profiles.
- For matching, evaluate stage, sector, support form, geography, financial basis and timing separately. Mandatory known failures cannot be canceled by a high semantic score. Missing data yields “needs confirmation,” and narrative criteria require manual review until approved mappings exist. The user should see a few fit reasons, the most important missing item and a direct next action.
- Cold-start ranking should work from explicit fields, editable preferences and curated records. A small corpus can evaluate all authorized pairs. Do not require engagement histories or train a proprietary recommender before useful data exists. Keep investor interest distinct from company directory presence: a public company page does not establish active investment intent.
- Preserve a minimal partner flow: create profile, choose offered support and accepted stages, publish an optional specific need, select intake route, then review submissions. Allow direct pitching only when the partner explicitly enables it. Honor external application routes rather than pretending a tracked external click is a submitted or accepted pitch. This follows the recipient-route diversity observed in [OpenVC](https://www.openvc.app/investor-outreach).

### Gaps

- No reliable source examined provides a universal investment-readiness score or probability of funding. Any internal fit index must be described as review prioritization, with separately displayed uncertainty.
- Currency conversion, debt, grant administration, share issuance, payments, custody, crowdfunding and securities execution were not designed or legally evaluated. The generic base can describe an intended support form without executing it.
- Product-specific restrictions, active investor availability, thesis accuracy, ownership targets and minimum entity requirements need verified partner input and expiry or review dates.

## What collaboration, confidentiality and UX behavior belongs in the minimum reusable product?

### Takeaway

Make the product a connected path from a safe public summary to a deliberate introduction, a shared review workspace and a mutually confirmed outcome. Progressive disclosure applies to both the interface and sensitive information; simplifying screens must not erase meaningful business states.

### Cited Findings

- WIPO distinguishes licensing, assignment, collaboration and confidentiality agreements. Licenses specify permitted rights and scope; assignments transfer ownership. Its NDA discussion emphasizes defining protected information, access and purpose. It also says model agreements need adaptation to the circumstances. This supports separate relationship types and deliberate document access; a platform's “confirmed” badge is not an executed license, an NDA, legal due diligence or a transfer of rights. — [WIPO, Technology Transfer Agreements](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/agreements)
- Halo's opportunity course describes filtering requests by technology, partnership model and eligible partner criteria, reading benefits and deadlines, asking questions, saving proposal progress, tracking active and archived proposals and communicating through an inbox. These are observable task patterns useful for a connected application, rather than evidence that users need a large dashboard. — [Halo, Finding and Applying to Opportunities](https://knowledge.halo.science/articles/4031823346-finding-and-applying-to-opportunities-on-halo)
- FirstIgnite advertises a progression linking company discovery, outreach and management of licensing conversations, including NDA, evaluation, negotiation and executed-license stages. Its product page identifies fragmented contact and agreement records as a problem it aims to address. These are vendor-described functions; marketing claims of speed or outcome improvement were not validated. — [FirstIgnite, Technology Transfer](https://firstignite.com/solutions/technology-transfer)
- WIPO GREEN's toolkit describes a catalogue of technologies and needs, including prototypes and marketable products offered for collaboration, licensing, joint ventures or sale. Seekers include companies, institutions and NGOs. Its upload advice emphasizes understandable language, a clear problem and pictures. This supports a problem-led asset profile with multiple possible support routes rather than a publication catalogue alone. — [WIPO GREEN, Partnership Toolkit](https://www3.wipo.int/wipogreen/en/toolkit/)

### Inferences

- Present the invention page in a task-oriented order: problem and intended users; what exists now; support sought; stage and evidence; team and authority; optional financial scope; consented relationships. Keep current capabilities separate from proposed development. A patent, video, revenue figure, institutional affiliation or regulated-sector approval must not be universally required.
- Use a short staged editor with visible saved state and a public preview. Require a nonconfidential summary for public discovery; keep detailed files private by default. Sharing requires a named recipient or workspace, selected file versions, clear purpose and an explicit action. A confidentiality acknowledgement can record a step, but should not impersonate an executed NDA or promise that a recipient cannot retain prior downloads. This is a product interpretation of [WIPO's agreement guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/agreements).
- Reuse the original PRD's strong workflow semantics: immutable submission snapshots; approve for discussion, decline or request itemized revision; answers per revision; scheduling with timezone-aware slots and counterproposals; one discussion workspace per pitch; a timeline showing who acts next. A passed meeting time must not automatically mean the meeting occurred. An accepted pitch must not automatically create a partnership.
- Keep outcome confirmation separate. One party proposes the relationship summary, the other confirms the exact same version and permitted public fields. Changes invalidate prior version confirmations. Retain evidence labels such as self-declared, source-reported and reviewed within a stated scope. Both-party confirmation means both parties agreed to that recorded summary; it does not mean independent legal or financial verification.
- Make recoverable states part of the PRD: saved drafts, no matches, incomplete eligibility, stale requirements, denied document access, duplicate retries, interrupted uploads, conflicting booking and withdrawn consent. Preserve user input and offer one useful recovery action. Start home screens with “Waiting for you” and “Waiting for the other party,” while keeping detailed matching numbers secondary.
- For a cross-sector generic fixture set, use clearly fictional inventions and partners: for example an irrigation controller, a reusable packaging process, a software scheduling tool and an assistive device. Include an independent inventor with no entity or patent, a company fundraising across two inventions, an NGO seeking validation support, and a partner accepting only a later stage. These are proposed test cases, not market validation or real partner interest.

### Gaps

- No usability study establishes the ideal navigation, field count, upload limit, ranking threshold or SLA. Keep proposed defaults labeled and test task completion, explanation comprehension and misunderstanding of funding or confirmation states.
- This assignment did not evaluate accessibility conformance, runtime authorization, malware scanning, model quality, third-party integrations or operational security. Those require implementation-level evidence.
- The comparison does not justify a social feed, public inventor ratings, mass outreach, cap-table management, generic legal-document generator, complex calendar application or enterprise CRM in the first product base.
